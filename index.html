01 / The Group

One connected ecosystem. Multiple dimensions of expertise.

ECC Group brings together nine specialist businesses operating across the construction and built-environment value chain.

From contracting and electromechanical engineering to fit-out, manufacturing, property development, facilities management and prefabricated solutions, each business contributes its own specialist expertise.
