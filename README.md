# ECC Group

## Website Overview

ECC Group is a diversified UAE-based business group with more than 50 years of experience in the construction and built-environment industry. Established through Engineering Contracting Company in 1975, the Group brings together nine specialist businesses covering construction, engineering, manufacturing, interior fit-out, real estate development, facilities management and modular solutions.

The redesigned homepage positions ECC Group as an integrated business ecosystem, helping investors, clients, developers and industry partners understand the relationship between its nine companies, explore its capabilities and discover relevant projects.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Nine-Business Structure

- Introduced a more organised overview of the nine specialist businesses.
- Provided clearer descriptions of each company's services and areas of expertise.
- Improved the presentation of how the subsidiaries contribute to the wider ECC Group ecosystem.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify the appropriate company for their requirements.

### 2. Stronger Corporate Credibility

- Improved the presentation of key group achievements, including 50+ years of experience and the stated AED 34 billion project portfolio.
- Distinguished group-level statistics from individual subsidiary achievements.
- Structured company credentials, certifications and operational capabilities with clearer attribution.
- Strengthened the presentation of the Group's history and established industry presence.

### 3. Improved Project Portfolio Discovery

- Organised project presentation to make the Group's experience easier to explore.
- Introduced clearer project descriptions and relevant business attribution.
- Improved connections between projects, industry sectors and the companies responsible for delivering them.
- Focused on helping visitors understand the Group's capabilities through its project experience.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more structured corporate identity that reflects ECC Group's scale, experience and diversified business operations.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and organisation across the Group overview, subsidiaries, achievements and project portfolio.
- **User Experience:** Made subsidiary discovery and navigation between different business areas more straightforward.
- **Content Consistency:** Created a more coherent presentation of company profiles, capabilities, credentials and project information.
- **Information Architecture:** Connected the Group's nine businesses through a clearer corporate structure while maintaining their individual identities.

## Overall Direction

The redesigned homepage positions ECC Group as a connected corporate ecosystem rather than simply a collection of separate companies. It provides a clearer introduction to the Group's nine businesses, strengthens the presentation of its collective experience and makes its project portfolio more accessible to clients, investors and industry partners.
